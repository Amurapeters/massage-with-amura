About Amura

25 years of experience

Originally trained as a physiotherapist, I worked as a massage therapist at a retreat centre in Dorset for 20 years, eventually becoming head of their wellbeing department. As well as giving sessions on a daily basis, I also ran workshops and trainings in the wellbeing field.

As well as my background in physiotherapy, I am also influenced by Hawaiian bodywork and Esalen massage which both use long strokes and stretches. By soothing the body and the nervous system, my aim is to help you feel relaxed, rejuvenated and whole.
